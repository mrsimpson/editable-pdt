# Learning Engine

How the platform helps its peers grow beyond transacting.

## From market stall to planned harvest

Most farms start with a market stall and a guess. The engine takes them step by step to a harvest
that is sold before it is sown.

```pdt
:::learning-engine
id: le-farmers
title: From market stall to planned harvest
entity: e-farmers
current: Sells what happens to grow, throws away a fifth
desired: Plants to committed demand, co-owns the platform
steps: s-storefront, s-demand-insights, s-planning-circles, s-membership
:::
```
