# Platform

Harvest Commons connects the small farms around the city with the households and kitchens
that want to eat what grows nearby. It started as a Saturday market stall shared by four farms
and is becoming a cooperative platform owned by its producers.

## Harvest Commons

Local food is not scarce. What is scarce is **coordination**: farmers guess what to plant,
households cannot plan around what is available, and every farm drives its own van into town.
Harvest Commons turns guesses into commitments. Households subscribe ahead, restaurants pre-order
by the season, and the cooperative bundles delivery so that no farm drives alone.
