# Motivations

What each entity offers the others. Read each block as "from gives to".

## Farmers and households

Farmers bring the freshness, households bring the commitment that makes planning possible.

```pdt
:::motivation
id: m-farmers-households
from: e-farmers
to: e-households
gives: Produce harvested the day before pick-up
:::

:::motivation
id: m-households-farmers
from: e-households
to: e-farmers
gives: A season-long subscription to plan against
:::
```
