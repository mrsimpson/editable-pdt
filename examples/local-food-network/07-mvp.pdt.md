# MVP

The smallest tests that tell whether the experiences work.

## Twelve-week box pilot

Four farms, one courier and three hubs in one neighbourhood for one summer.

```pdt
:::mvp
id: mvp-box-pilot
title: Twelve-week box pilot
experience: x-weekly-box
status: running
hypotheses:
  - Households subscribe for a whole season
  - Farms can forecast a week ahead
  - A hub within 500 m is close enough
experiments:
  - Pilot in one neighbourhood, three hubs
  - Spreadsheet forecast before building the app
metrics:
  - Subscription retention after 12 weeks
  - Share of boxes picked up on time
  - Waste per farm, before and after
criteria:
  - 70 % renew after 12 weeks
  - Waste halves for pilot farms
:::
```

## Winter planning circle

One round of crop planning with three chefs and three farms before the next season.

```pdt
:::mvp
id: mvp-chefs-circle
title: Winter planning circle
experience: x-chefs-table
status: planned
hypotheses:
  - Chefs commit to volumes five months ahead
experiments:
  - Two planning evenings in the college kitchen
metrics:
  - Volume pre-ordered per restaurant
criteria:
  - Each chef commits to at least three varieties
:::
```
