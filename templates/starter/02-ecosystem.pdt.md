# Ecosystem

Who takes part in the ecosystem? One section per entity; `role` places it on the ecosystem canvas.

## Platform Owner

Who shapes and governs the platform?

```pdt
:::entity
id: e-owner
title: Platform Owner
role: shaper
pressures:
  - What pushes them to build the platform?
gains:
  - What do they get out of it?
:::
```

## Producers

Who creates the value that is exchanged?

```pdt
:::entity
id: e-producers
title: Producers
role: peer-producer
context:
  - Who they are, where they operate
pressures:
  - Performance pressures and trends
gains:
  - What they hope to gain
seeks:
  - The experience they are looking for
resources:
  - What they bring and could share
:::
```

## Consumers

Who consumes that value?

```pdt
:::entity
id: e-consumers
title: Consumers
role: peer-consumer
pressures:
  - What makes today's alternative painful?
gains:
  - What would make them switch?
:::
```
